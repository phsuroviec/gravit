## The rule

A fold is inert while carried and live while placed — you can never be inside
the gravity you are holding, and no fold can ever be lost. E picks up (scope
off), E sets down (scope on). X is deleted from input, HUD and docs.

## The room (room1.tscn, the only room)

- Beat 1, Throw: carry the cyan (east) fold over the shelf crate, set it down.
  The crate slides east onto a 5 m landing strip (a fixed throw always lands
  on it regardless of where the player stands). Pad queues the impulse, the
  door slides west out of its frame after 1.6 s.
- Beat 2, Ride: the green (up) fold stands live east of the shelf. Walk in, it
  lifts you, drift east at the top onto the ledge, walk through the open door
  into the exit. One airlock terminal (room1, keeps the T narrator + Meta
  runtime-call path), two billboard plates, warm key over the shelf, cool key
  over the ledge, fog + glow in the course environment.

  glides: `place()` now reconciles membership from `get_overlapping_bodies()`
  (add-only; real exits still arrive via body_exited).
- The pad answered to the player too, so the puzzle was bypassable on foot:
  pads now ignore the player group.
- The east wall had no jambs: the 1.6 m door left walk-around gaps. Jambs now
  leave a 1.8 m opening the capsule cannot bypass.
- door.tscn axis locks only allow x-slide: the room's impulse is (-5,0,0),
  variant rooms keep the default (6,0,0). Both pass.
- `player.gd` was truncated mid-surgery (move_and_slide lost, body frozen with
  velocity set but never integrated). Rebuilt the tail from 76e53b0 minus the
  ladder hack and the X verb.
