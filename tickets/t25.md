## The chain (bot-proven, honest input throughout)

Place east fold at the ledge edge → grab up fold → ride east carrying it

## Hard findings (grill-verified, then bot-verified)

- Instant flip + normal acceleration is exactly what the code does — there is
  no dwell mechanic and none was added. Height comes from time-inside, which
  is why fast entries give flat line drives and only slow/braked entries
  moonshot. The design is honest about that instead of fighting it.
- A floor 2 m fold caps moonshots ~1.5 m above entry: undetectable from a
  jump. The up fold is 2 × 3 × 2 (one metre of extra height buys the entire
  jump-proof margin: jump top 3.52 can never touch the 3.875+ layer).
- Marginal capsule/face overlaps flicker membership; decisive penetration is
  stable. The bot walks in at full speed, brakes once deep (2 frames), then
  releases — no stutter (its phase alignment jittered the apex ±0.3).
- Touchdown apex is deterministic to the millimetre across runs (3.116653);
  near-misses retry by walking back in (fold stays placed, nothing resets).

## Camera + body (ADR-11)

One eased orientation state drives both: ease toward a fold's down on ENTRY
(~0.6 s, slow-start like gravity), HOLD through airborne exits (no strobing —
the moonshot stays inverted), ease back upright only on grounded touchdown.
Facing (yaw + stored pitch) is rebuilt deterministically every frame, so
nothing drifts mid-flip. FOV kick + whoosh still fire on entry. Head mesh
hidden (FPS standard: the camera is the head).
