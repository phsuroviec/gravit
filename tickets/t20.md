Picking a fold up was disorienting: the 2 m shell teleported to a point 1.5 m in front of the
camera, stayed fully opaque, and nothing confirmed the grab. There was also no way to tell which
fold E would take when two were close, and the reach rule silently allowed ~4 m.

## Changes

- Held fold floats 2 m ahead, 0.6 m below eye level, 0.4 m right (`Player.hand_point`), pulled in
  by a raycast so it stops at walls but never closer than 1.5 m. It **glides** there at 12/s instead
  of teleporting, and its membrane dims to 0.1 alpha with low emission while carried so it stops
  fogging the view. Hold distance stays under a fold's own width: the drop point must remain inside
  the scope you stand in, or room 3's break hands its cube to the wrong gravity.
- Two viewmodel hands (`Player/Camera3D/CarryHands`) appear while carrying; a ground ring marks the
  fold E would take, and only that one.
- Shell and particle colour follow the fold's gravity direction (`FoldBox.tint_for`), so two folds
  side by side no longer read as one object. Shells draw back faces only, so a fold you stand in
  does not fog your view.
- Reach is measured to the shell surface (3 m), replacing the old buggy near-tie clause that let a
  grab happen at ~4 m. Deepest fold still wins ties.
- Set-down drops the fold where it floats (`FoldBox.drop`), not at a fixed anchor.
- Grab blip (`Sfx.grab`) + pad haptic; HUD prompt now reads the player's own target, so the ring
  and the prompt cannot disagree.
- `docs/folds-explained.md`: what a fold is, its four abilities, the boundaries, the pad, and the
  goal/objective chain — the designer primer that was missing.
