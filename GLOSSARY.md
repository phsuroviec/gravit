# Glossary — Gravit stunt level

Short names for the things level 1 is made of. Use these in tickets and docs.

- **Sling** — the fixed launcher fold. Gravity east (+X). You run in, it throws you.
- **Uplift** — the fixed upper fold. Gravity up. Entered mid-flight, exits upward-fast.
- **Gate** — the glowing recess high on the east wall. Visual promise of the next room, always open.
- **Platform** — the slab on top of the gate frame. Land on it and the run completes (touchdown wins).
- **Touchdown** — the thin exit layer just under the skyblock. Touch wins mid-air.
- **Moonshot** — a slow braked entry into the up fold: dwell becomes height.
- **Line drive** — a fast entry: through the side, flat and far, never high.
- **Fixed fold** — a fold with `fixed = true`: architecture, not an object. E ignores it.
- **Air inertia** — flight keeps its velocity (light drag); ground still snaps to input.
- **Flip cue** — the FOV kick + whoosh fired when your up changes.
